Questions, Answered

Common questions before we kick off.

Quick answers to what most brands ask in our first call. Got something else? Drop us a line.

Ask Us Anything
What types of brands do you typically work with?
We work primarily with wellness, health, skincare, nutrition, and fitness brands across India — from D2C startups to established names like Minimalist, Terra Origin, Chemist At Play, Dot & Key, and Food Strong.
How do you choose the right creator for our brand?
Every creator is vetted on audience authenticity, engagement quality, and product fit — not just follower count. Doctors, nutritionists, fitness coaches, and skin experts in our network are matched by category, tone, and conversion history.
What's the typical timeline from brief to launch?
From brief to creator-match in 48 hours; from match to live campaign in 2–3 weeks depending on scope. Bigger productions or cross-creator collabs take 4–6 weeks.
Do you handle reporting and performance tracking?
Yes. Every campaign comes with reach, engagement, sentiment, and conversion reporting. We focus on metrics that actually correlate to revenue, not vanity numbers.
Can we work on a long-term creator partnership?
Absolutely — community building is where we shine. Always-on creator partnerships outperform one-off campaigns by a wide margin, and we structure deals that work for both sides.
